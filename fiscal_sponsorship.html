What It Means

Funding-ready, without the red tape

Fiscal sponsorship allows your project or program to operate under the legal and tax-exempt umbrella of CBO HubSpace. Funders make grants and donors give to CBO HubSpace on your behalf, and those dollars flow directly to your work — fully accounted for and compliant.

This is ideal for emerging initiatives, grassroots efforts, and established programs that want to access funding opportunities reserved for registered 501(c)(3) organizations without the cost and delay of incorporating one.

Important: Members-Only Submission

CBO HubSpace submits grant proposals on behalf of members only. Non-members are welcome to apply and receive proposal preparation support, but must submit their own proposals. Becoming a member unlocks full end-to-end submission handled by our team. Not sure? You can indicate your interest in membership right on the application.
