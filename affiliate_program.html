The Process

A straightforward path to affiliate status

Membership at CBO Hub is the gateway. Once enrolled, your congregation becomes an affiliate network with income-generating potential from day one.

01
Join CBO Hub

Your church or organization enrolls as a CBO Hub member, gaining immediate access to low-cost programs, services, and the affiliate framework.

02
Activate Affiliate Status

Member churches are automatically recognized as affiliates. You receive a dedicated pipeline to sign up additional CBO Hub members within your community.

03
Earn Referral Fees

Each qualified member you bring into the platform generates a referral fee for your organization, structured transparently in the Affiliate Fee Chart.

Coaching Per Member

Each individual you refer receives dedicated coaching, creating measurable outcomes and community-level accountability.

Coaching is delivered so that the mission remains aligned.

CBO Hub does not operate as a passive referral network. Every member your organization enrolls receives structured coaching delivered in coordination with your church — creating a unified direction rather than fragmented participation.

This alignment model ensures that revenue generation and mission integrity operate in parallel, not in tension. Your church grows its community impact while building a revenue stream that reinvests directly into that community.

Both member and non-member pipelines are available to affiliates, giving you flexibility in outreach while maintaining the pricing advantages that belong exclusively to enrolled members.

Designated Use of Proceeds

Revenue that serves the community

Referral fees earned through the affiliate program are intended for direct community benefit. Three approved designations ensure funds remain mission-aligned.

Church Operating Expenses

Apply referral income to facility costs, administrative overhead, ministry operations, or any qualified organizational expense necessary to sustain church functions.

Member Scholarships

Fund scholarship programs that provide qualified congregants with access to CBO Hub services and educational programming at reduced or no cost.

Program Subsidies

Subsidize participation in CBO Hub programs for members who face financial barriers — expanding community access and reinforcing the stewardship mission.
