1. Location Partner Expansion Plan

Executive Summary

GLCC operates as a “crossroads” hub in Milwaukee’s 53206 corridor, integrating healthcare, community programming, and institutional partnerships. This plan outlines a 12-month pathway to reach $30,000/month through diversified revenue streams.

Six core revenue streams: Fiscal Sponsorship, Space Rental, Grants, Academic Partnerships, Memberships, and Research/Training Contracts.

Core Strategy

  • Activate earned revenue first (space + sponsorship)
  • Layer institutional partnerships (academic + research)
  • Treat grants as supplemental
  • Scale to full capacity by Month 10–12

Key Assets

  • Living Well Clinic + Counseling
  • Academic presence (MCW, UWM)
  • Memory Café + County partnerships
  • Event + training + fitness space

Target Outcome

$25K–$30K+/month by Year 1 completion

2. Revenue Mix Analysis

Facility Context

40,000 SF facility with underutilized space representing major upside.

12,000–18,000 SF underutilized = future $96K–$216K/year opportunity

Four Core Revenue Engines

  • Space + Hosting: $8K–$10K/month (anchor)
  • Training / Research: $6K–$8K/month
  • Fiscal Sponsorship: $6K–$8K/month
  • Membership: $2.5K–$3.5K/month

Key Insight

The model works only when revenue streams are combined — not isolated.

Operational Reality

  • Low utilization threshold = high upside
  • Recurring contracts outperform hourly bookings
  • Fiscal sponsorship scales only after 5–8 projects
