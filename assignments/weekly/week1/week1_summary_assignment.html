The Challenge
Mission Misalignment Drives Measurable Participation Decline
63%
of church ministry programs have not been updated in over a decade to reflect the current community context surrounding the congregation.
Source: Barna Group — State of the Church, 2022

When a congregation’s programs and mission statement no longer reflect the demographic, economic, or cultural reality of its surrounding neighborhood, participation erodes — not because people have lost faith, but because the institution has lost contextual relevance. The Hartford Institute for Religion Research identifies mission-community alignment as one of the top three predictors of congregational vitality. Pew Research Center (2023) reports that 28% of Americans who disengaged from their church cite institutional disconnection from community needs as a contributing factor. Additionally, Hartford Institute data reveals that over 40% of U.S. congregations have experienced significant neighborhood demographic shifts since founding, yet fewer than 15% have formally updated their mission focus. Alignment is not aspirational — it is foundational to sustained participation.

Week 1 Assignment
Activity Focus
Church History & Identity Research
Members’ Review — July Monthly Meeting
1
Locate Founding Documents
Retrieve your church’s original articles of incorporation, charter, or founding mission statement. Compare it word-for-word to the current stated mission. Document every gap in writing.
2
Research Neighborhood Demographics
Trace neighborhood demographics from the church’s inception to present using U.S. Census records, city planning data, or available community demographic profiles.
3
Conduct Structured Interviews
Interview the church historian, founding members, or the senior pastor. Ask: What problem were we founded to solve? Who were we serving? What ministries existed at inception?
4
Google Maps Neighborhood Snapshot
Use Google Maps historical satellite imagery to capture a snapshot of the church’s immediate neighborhood every 5 years — from inception or 2007 to the present. Document visible shifts.
5
Synthesize & Document Findings
Write a structured summary identifying: original mission, current mission, neighborhood demographic shift, and the alignment gap between founding purpose and present-day community context.
6
Post Results for Community Engagement
Share research discoveries, interview insights, and Google Maps comparisons in the comments of this week’s social media posts. Community responses are part of the learning framework.
💬
Share on Social Media
Post findings, interview highlights, and Google Maps comparisons in the comments of this week’s CBO HubSpace social posts. Community engagement is intentional — it drives peer learning across congregations.
🔒
Members-Only Assignment Review
The full assignment review, peer analysis, and strategic frameworks for realigning mission with community context are conducted exclusively in the July CBO HubSpace monthly members’ meeting.
