Member Pricing is Exclusive

Discounted rates apply only to enrolled CBO Hub members. Non-member pricing applies to all other participants, creating a clear incentive for your congregation to join.

Referral fees are earned at the point of each qualified enrollment. Annual coaching packages generate $200 per referral — significantly accelerating income at volume.

Proceeds Serve Your Mission

Affiliate income may be applied to church operating expenses, member scholarships, or program subsidies — keeping revenue circulating within the community you serve.
