Membership

Join a professional ecosystem designed for providers committed to sustainable, community-centered practice.

Why Membership

Built for Providers. Designed for Growth.

Membership provides access to structured space, consistent scheduling, and integration into a professional wellness environment. Members receive priority access to bookable spaces and become part of a coordinated provider ecosystem.

Allocated Monthly Access

Reserved hours and blocks aligned to your practice needs — no competing for availability.

Priority Scheduling

Members receive first access to prime-time availability before non-member requests are reviewed.

Ecosystem Integration

Connect with providers, educators, and partners operating within the hub.

Operational Support

Administrative infrastructure that supports your daily workflow and scheduling coordination.

Expansion Opportunities

Members gain access to CBO HubSpace partnership opportunities as the facility expands.

Community Impact

Operate within a mission-aligned environment serving Milwaukee's most underserved communities.
