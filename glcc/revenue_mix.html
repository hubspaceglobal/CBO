Three Structural Conclusions

  • Space fees are the anchor. 168 hours of room use/month across 4 room types at blended rates generates $12,000+. That is roughly 2 hours per room per weekday — a low utilization bar for 40,000 SF. Evenings and weekends are the highest-demand periods; price at the standard rate, not community rate.
  • Fiscal sponsorship is most powerful as a combined offer. 10% fee alone builds slowly. When a sponsored project also rents space, a single $80K/year project generates $15,800 combined. Recruit projects that will use the building, not just pass funds through remotely.
  • Grants are supplemental — not structural. The $30K/month target should be achievable entirely from earned income by Month 11–12. Any grant revenue received goes to margin, program capacity, or operating reserves. Treating grants as core operating revenue creates structural fragility.
