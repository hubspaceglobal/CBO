Logo Construction

The GLCC mark is built on a precise geometric intersection. Two equal-weight lines cross at a defined center point. The intersection node is marked with a two-layer circle: the outer ring in Warm Amber, the inner point in the background color. This construction is consistent across all mark sizes. The minimum clear space around the logo is equal to the diameter of the amber node on all sides.

  • Never place Amber text on Navy — contrast is insufficient for body copy

Motif Application Rules

The crossroads geometry is the visual foundation of the entire system. It appears as the logo mark, as a background grid pattern (at reduced opacity, 4-8%), as a compositional quadrant structure in layouts, and as a node-and-line system for diagrams and program maps. The intersection node — always in Warm Amber — is the focal anchor in every application. The motif is never used decoratively without structural purpose.

05 — Spacing System

Layout Grid

All layouts are built on a base-8 spacing unit. Print layouts use a 12-column grid with 24px gutters. Digital layouts use a 12-column grid with 16px gutters. All compositions follow the quadrant-structure principle derived from the crossroads motif.

06 — Photography

Image Direction

Interaction

Human Interaction

Provider and patient. Elder and youth. Researcher and resident. Frame the meeting point — the moment of connection — not the individual in isolation.

Environment

Space and Environment

Real spaces, real activity. The facility in use — not empty. Doorways, thresholds, gathering moments. Avoid sterile or staged room shots.

Community

Community Presence

Authentic community life. Documentary quality, not charity marketing. Subjects are participants — not recipients. Dignity is non-negotiable in every frame.

Color Grading

  • Cool-toned overall grading consistent with the palette
  • High contrast — deep shadows, clear highlights
  • Documentary quality — not filtered or over-processed
  • Desaturated backgrounds with subject clarity
  • Amber and warm skin tones preserved — not neutralized

Avoid

  • Stock photography — use original photography where possible
  • Posed group shots with generic expressions
  • Imagery that reads as charity or poverty marketing
  • Oversaturated or heavily filtered images
  • Empty rooms presented as program evidence

Horizontal Co-Brand

  • GLCC lockup leads on the left in all co-branded materials
  • Vertical divider separates marks — minimum 24px clear space each side
  • Partner logo appears at equal visual weight — not scaled smaller

Co-Brand Rules

  • GLCC brand elements are never altered to match a partner palette
  • Partner logos are placed on GLCC-approved background colors only
  • No partner logo may be larger than the GLCC primary lockup in GLCC-produced materials
  • In partner-produced materials, provide the GLCC logo suite file — never allow recreation
  • CBO Hub carries its own separate identity and is not co-branded under GLCC visual standards
  • All co-branded materials require GLCC approval before distribution

09 — Usage Standards

Do and Do Not

Do

Use Deep Navy or Steel Blue as the primary background in all digital and print communications.

Do

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the diameter of the Amber node on all sides.

Do

Use Barlow Condensed for all headlines, display text, and labels. Use Inter for all body text.

Do

Apply the crossroads motif as a structural element — grid background, quadrant layout, or diagram node system.

Do Not

Do not alter the logo colors, proportions, or mark construction under any circumstances.

Do Not

Do not use Warm Amber as a dominant background or primary color. It is an accent element only.

Do Not

Do not use decorative, script, or serif typefaces anywhere in the GLCC brand system.

Do Not

Do not place the logo on patterned backgrounds or photography without an approved solid-color field behind it.

Do Not

Do not recreate or redraw the logo from screen. Always use approved source files for reproduction.
