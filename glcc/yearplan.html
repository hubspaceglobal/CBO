SYSTEMS & INFRASTRUCTURE

  • CRM: Bloomerang / Little Green Light for donor + partner tracking
  • Grant Management: Airtable pipeline with submission, status, reporting tracking
  • Financial System: QuickBooks Nonprofit integrated with CRM
  • Agreement Management: DocuSign + centralized contract repository
  • Program Systems: Eventbrite / Forms for registration + Airtable tracking
  • Communication: Mailchimp (email), Slack or Google Workspace (internal)
  • Brand Assets: Canva or Adobe system for templates and collateral
  • Website + Platform: GLCC site + CBOHub integration
  • Data & Reporting: Standardized monthly reporting templates
  • Document Storage: Structured Drive system (legal, grants, programs, finance)

RISK MITIGATION

  • Capacity Risk (Q1): Limit scope to foundational work only; prioritize hiring timeline
  • Revenue Delay Risk: Prioritize earned income streams (space + sponsorship) early
  • Grant Dependency Risk: Treat grants as supplemental, not core operations
  • Partnership Execution Risk: Standardize MOU templates and tracking systems
  • Compliance Risk: Engage legal review for all agreements and fiscal sponsorships
  • Program Consistency Risk: Implement standardized schedules and facilitation guides
  • System Fragmentation Risk: Centralize all tools and data systems early (Q1–Q2)
  • Cash Flow Risk: Build recurring revenue (membership + rentals) before scaling staff
  • Market Risk: Diversify across 4 revenue streams to reduce dependency
  • Scaling Risk: Sequence growth based on staffing—not demand alone
